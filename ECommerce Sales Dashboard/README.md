# 🛒 Ecommerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

![Ecommerce Sales Dashboard](ECommerce Sales Dashboard/Images/Project-2_Image-1.png)

## 📌 Introduction

This Power BI dashboard was built to analyze ecommerce sales performance across product categories, customer segments, regions, and shipping types. It provides a single, easy-to-use interface for business stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

The primary goal of this project is to transform raw ecommerce sales data into a visually compelling and interactive dashboard that answers key business questions around revenue, profitability, product performance, and customer behaviour.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### Key KPIs
| Metric | Value | YoY Change |
|---|---|---|
| YTD Sales | $11.53M | -0.83% ▼ |
| YTD Profit | 1.34M | +4.50% ▲ |
| YTD Quantity | 107K | -7.29% ▼ |
| YTD Profit Margin | 11.58% | +5.37% ▲ |

---

## 🔍 Analysis Sections

### 1. Sales by Category
Comparison of YTD vs PYTD sales with Year-over-Year growth across:
- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

### 2. Top 5 & Bottom 5 Products by YTD Sales
Identifies best and worst performing products to support inventory and marketing decisions.

### 3. YTD Sales by Customer Region
Donut chart breakdown of sales across Central, East, South, and West regions.

### 4. Sales by State
Geospatial map visualization showing sales distribution across US states by customer region.

### 5. YTD Sales by Shipping Type
Analysis of revenue contribution by different shipping methods.

## ⚙️ Skills Showcased

- **ETL with Power Query** — Data cleaning, type conversion, and feature creation
- **DAX Measures** — KPI calculations for YTD, PYTD, YoY, and Profit Margin
- **Core Charts** — Bar, Column, Line, and Donut charts
- **Geospatial Analysis** — Map chart for regional sales distribution
- **KPI Cards** — Trend indicators with sparklines
- **Interactive Reporting** — Slicers for Customer Segment filtering
- **Dashboard Design** — Clean, dark-themed layout for executive reporting

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

- **Source:** Ecommerce sales transaction data
- **Scope:** Multiple product categories, US regions, customer segments
- **Key Fields:** Sales, Profit, Quantity, Category, Region, Shipping Type, Product Name

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Office Supplies** contributes the highest YTD sales at $6.92M but shows a -1.22% YoY decline
- **Furniture** is the only category with positive YoY growth at +0.73%
- **West region** leads with 32.22% share of total YTD sales
- **Standard shipping** dominates at 60.51% of total sales by shipping type
- Despite a drop in overall sales and quantity, **profit margin improved by 5.37%**, indicating better cost efficiency
